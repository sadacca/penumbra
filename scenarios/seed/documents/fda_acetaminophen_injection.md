## Boxed Warning: Risk of Hepatotoxicity

Acetaminophen has been associated with cases of acute liver failure, at times
resulting in liver transplant and death. Most cases of liver injury are
associated with the use of acetaminophen at doses that exceed the maximum
recommended daily amount, and often involve more than one acetaminophen-
containing product.

## Overdosage

**Presentation.** In acute overdose, hepatotoxicity may occur. Early symptoms
(first 24 hours) are nonspecific: nausea, vomiting, diaphoresis, and malaise.
Clinical and laboratory evidence of hepatic injury may not be apparent until
48 to 72 hours after ingestion.

**Management.** Treatment includes prompt assessment of the ingested amount,
serum acetaminophen concentration measured at the appropriate interval, and
administration of acetylcysteine as the antidote according to the established
dosing nomogram. Supportive care and specialist consultation are advised.
