## Facts

A police detective observed individuals repeatedly walking past and peering
into a store window in a manner he understood, from long experience, to be
consistent with casing the premises for a robbery. He approached, identified
himself, and conducted a limited pat-down of their outer clothing, discovering
concealed weapons.

## Standard

Where a police officer observes unusual conduct which leads him reasonably to
conclude in light of his experience that criminal activity may be afoot, and
that the persons may be armed and dangerous, the officer may conduct a limited
search of the outer clothing to discover weapons. The standard is whether a
reasonably prudent officer would be warranted in the belief that his safety or
that of others was in danger.

## Holding

Such a limited search ("stop and frisk") is a reasonable search under the
Fourth Amendment, and weapons seized may be introduced in evidence. The
inquiry is an objective one based on specific and articulable facts, not an
officer's inchoate and unparticularized suspicion or hunch.
