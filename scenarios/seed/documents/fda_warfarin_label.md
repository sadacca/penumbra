## Boxed Warning: Bleeding Risk

Warfarin sodium can cause major or fatal bleeding. Bleeding is more likely to
occur during the starting period and with a higher dose (resulting in a higher
INR). Regular monitoring of INR should be performed on all treated patients.

## Drug and Food Interactions (selected)

Many drugs and foods alter warfarin response. Inhibitors of CYP2C9, CYP1A2, or
CYP3A4 may increase warfarin exposure and bleeding risk; inducers may decrease
it. Increased dietary intake of vitamin K can reduce anticoagulant effect.
Concomitant antiplatelet agents or NSAIDs increase bleeding risk without
necessarily changing the INR.
