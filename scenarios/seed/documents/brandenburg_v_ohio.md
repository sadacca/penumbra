## Holding and Test

The constitutional guarantees of free speech and free press do not permit a
State to forbid or proscribe advocacy of the use of force or of law violation
except where such advocacy is directed to inciting or producing imminent
lawless action and is likely to incite or produce such action. A statute that
fails to distinguish mere advocacy from incitement to imminent lawless action
sweeps within its condemnation speech that the Constitution protects.

## Disposition

The Ohio Criminal Syndicalism statute, under which the appellant was convicted,
punished mere advocacy and was therefore held unconstitutional. The conviction
was reversed.
