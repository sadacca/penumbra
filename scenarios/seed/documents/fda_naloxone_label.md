## Indications

Naloxone nasal spray is indicated for the emergency treatment of known or
suspected opioid overdose, as manifested by respiratory and/or central nervous
system depression. It is intended for use by laypersons as well as healthcare
providers.

## Dosage and Administration

Administer a single spray into one nostril. Seek emergency medical assistance
immediately. If the person does not respond, additional doses may be given
every 2 to 3 minutes, using a new device in alternating nostrils, until
emergency help arrives. Because the effect of naloxone may wear off before the
opioid effect, the person must be monitored continuously for re-emergence of
overdose symptoms.
