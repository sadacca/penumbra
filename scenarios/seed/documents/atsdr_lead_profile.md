## Health Effects of Lead Exposure (excerpt)

**Neurological.** Lead is a well-established neurotoxicant. In children, even
low blood lead levels are associated with deficits in cognitive function,
attention, and academic achievement; no threshold for these effects has been
identified. In adults, higher exposures are associated with peripheral
neuropathy and cognitive decline.

**Haematological.** Lead interferes with heme synthesis by inhibiting key
enzymes, contributing to anemia at higher exposure levels.

**Developmental.** Prenatal exposure is associated with reduced birth weight
and impaired neurodevelopment. Lead crosses the placenta and is mobilized from
maternal bone stores during pregnancy.
